🔍 Key Discovery Takeaways

  • Cognitive Friction: Users felt overwhelmed by fragmented multi-step procedures without clear status feedback.
  • Information Hierarchy: Critical action buttons lacked prominence, leading to high drop-off rates on mobile viewports.
  • Contextual Needs: Users demanded proactive guidance and smart defaults rather than reading complex documentation.
03 / Process

Design Process & Methodology

A structured Double Diamond framework guiding the journey from problem exploration to iterative solution delivery.

Phase 1: Discover & Empathize

Conducted stakeholder alignment workshops, 1-on-1 contextual inquiries, and comprehensive competitor benchmarking.

Phase 2: Define & Frame

Mapped user journey blueprints, established personas, and formulated focused "How Might We" challenge statements.

Phase 3: Ideate & Prototype

Explored wireframes, built interactive Figma component prototypes, and ran rapid usability testing loops.

Phase 4: Deliver & Measure

Partnered with engineering for pixel-perfect design QA, tokenized UI implementation, and post-launch analytics tracking.
